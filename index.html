Project overview

Designed around a real business need.

Repetitive messages can take time away from important work. A well-planned chatbot workflow can help businesses respond consistently to common enquiries while keeping human support available for more complex requests.

01 / The challenge

Answering the same questions.

Customers often ask about prices, products, opening hours, and orders. Responding manually to every routine question can slow down day-to-day operations.

02 / The approach

A clearer way to handle enquiries.

Plan a messaging workflow that identifies common requests, provides relevant information, and directs conversations requiring personal attention to a team member.

Workflow

From incoming message to useful response.

The four main stages of a typical messaging automation setup.

01 — Receive

Customer message

A customer sends an enquiry through a configured messaging channel.

02 — Understand

Process the request

Configured rules or an AI model help identify what the customer needs.

03 — Respond

Prepare an answer

The workflow uses available business information to prepare a relevant response.

04 — Follow up

Take the next step

Where configured, the workflow can record an enquiry or route it to a person.
